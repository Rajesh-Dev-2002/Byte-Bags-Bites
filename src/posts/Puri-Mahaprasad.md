## What to look for

1. Ananda Bazaar

3. Khaja sweet
4. Jagannath Temple


## What foods are included?
- Rice (Anna): The main staple.
- Dal: Traditional lentil preparations.
- Vegetable dishes: Seasonal vegetables cooked with traditional spices.
- Khatta: A sweet-and-sour preparation.
- Kheeri: A traditional rice-and-milk dessert.
- Pitha: Traditional cakes or rice-based preparations, depending on the offerings.
The exact varieties change according to the offerings and the day.
